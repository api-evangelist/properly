# Properly

> Properly was a Toronto-based Canadian digital real estate brokerage founded in 2018 — one of Canada's only iBuyers before it pivoted to "Sale Assurance", a guaranteed-purchase backstop wrapped around online listing search and a machine-generated home valuation. It was acquired by Pine Canada Financial Corporation in October 2023 and the brand has since been fully absorbed. Properly publishes no API, no developer portal, and no machine-readable contract of any kind.

- Successor: Pine Canada Financial Corporation — https://www.pine.ca/real-estate

- RESO posture: not certified, not a RESO member. Listings reach the platform through licensed channels (CREA MLS attribution; listing media served from cdn.repliers.io, the CDN of licensed MLS data vendor Repliers).
